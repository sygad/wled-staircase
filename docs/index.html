WLED Staircase - Interactive Demo

Everything in this demo behaves the way the real thing does. It is designed to work at night only — override by pressing the Moon button to force it into night-time mode.

Staircase
Sweep

Instructions

  • Press the Top / Bottom buttons to simulate walking up or down the stairs.
  • Sweep and Rainbow presets are direction aware.
  • All turns on every step at once, in either direction.
  • Matrix and Disco are just for fun.

Press Alarm armed house to simulate the downstairs floor being armed, then press Top to see the stairs light up, warning you that downstairs is armed.
